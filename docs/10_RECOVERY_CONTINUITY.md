Recovery Continuity
1. Purpose

Recovery continuity preserves unresolved human needs beyond immediate incident response.

2. Required Distinctions
CONTACT_ATTEMPTED ≠ CONTACT_CONFIRMED
REFERRED_TO_SERVICE ≠ SERVICE_ACCEPTED
SERVICE_ACCEPTED ≠ NEED_RESOLVED
CASE_CLOSED ≠ ALL_NEEDS_RESOLVED
3. Recovery Case Requirements

A recovery case must preserve:

originating incident;
affected household identifier;
unresolved needs;
referrals;
contact history;
service outcomes;
closure basis;
reopening history.
4. Closure

Case closure requires:

closure authority;
closure reason;
unresolved-needs statement;
timestamp;
evidence.
5. Reopening

A case may be reopened when:

a need remains unresolved;
new evidence appears;
prior closure was incorrect;
a referral failed;
contact is re-established.
