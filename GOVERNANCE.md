Governance
Governing Principle

Humanitarian systems must preserve uncertainty, authority, evidence, safety conditions, and unresolved need throughout the complete response lifecycle.

Constitutional Distinctions

The project preserves:

REPORT ≠ VERIFICATION
ASSIGNMENT ≠ ELIGIBILITY
INVENTORY RECORD ≠ AVAILABILITY
AUTHORIZATION ≠ DISPATCH READINESS
INTENTION ≠ DELIVERY
REFERRAL ≠ FULFILLMENT
CASE CLOSURE ≠ NEED RESOLUTION
Governance Requirements
Missing information remains missing.
Estimates remain distinguishable from verified facts.
Authorization must be revalidated before action.
State changes must preserve prior evidence.
Failed and unknown outcomes must not be erased.
Human review must remain available for consequential decisions.
Synthetic data is mandatory during public prototype development.
External organizational relationships must not be overstated.
ORYNTH remains institutionally and technically separate.
No production claim may exceed verified evidence.
Change Governance

Material changes require:

stated purpose;
affected domain;
compatibility analysis;
test evidence;
ownership review;
privacy review;
external-affiliation review;
release classification.
