Privacy Policy
1. Prototype Data Boundary

This repository permits synthetic demonstration data only.

2. Prohibited Data

Do not commit:

real names;
home addresses;
telephone numbers;
email addresses;
medical records;
disability details tied to real individuals;
immigration information;
financial-assistance records;
volunteer background-check results;
government identifiers;
real case numbers;
internal humanitarian records;
credentials;
access tokens;
authentication secrets.
3. Identifier Standard

Synthetic persons and entities must use generated identifiers such as:

INC-000001
HH-000001
AP-000001
VOL-000001
RES-000001
PLAN-000001
DEC-000001
4. Data Classification
PUBLIC_SYNTHETIC
INTERNAL_SYNTHETIC
RESTRICTED_TEST
PROHIBITED_REAL_DATA

Only PUBLIC_SYNTHETIC and INTERNAL_SYNTHETIC are currently permitted.

5. Future Real-Data Requirement

Any future use of real data requires a separate authorization process covering:

lawful basis;
consent or institutional authority;
purpose limitation;
minimization;
retention;
encryption;
access control;
incident response;
deletion;
audit;
human-subject and legal review where applicable.
