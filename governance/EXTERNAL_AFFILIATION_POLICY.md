# External Affiliation Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy prevents unsupported public claims involving nonprofit, humanitarian, educational, commercial, governmental, or intergovernmental organizations.

## 2. Required Status Distinctions

The project must preserve the following distinctions:

```text
IDENTIFIED
RESEARCHED
OUTREACH_PLANNED
CONTACTED
DISCUSSION_ACTIVE
PILOT_PROPOSED
AUTHORIZED_COLLABORATOR
ACTIVE_PARTNER
FORMER_PARTNER
No status may be advanced without evidence.

3. Public Reference Rule

An organization may be referenced as an example of a humanitarian domain only when the repository clearly states that no affiliation is implied.

4. Prohibited Claims

Without written authorization, contributors may not describe an external organization as:

partner;
collaborator;
client;
customer;
sponsor;
supporter;
beneficiary;
adopter;
endorser;
funder;
authorized user.
5. Logo and Trademark Rule

External logos, marks, trade dress, internal screenshots, or protected organizational materials may not be added without written authorization.

6. Required Disclaimer

Any external-facing demonstration must state:

This is an independent prototype. References to external organizations identify public humanitarian domains or prospective areas of outreach only. No partnership, endorsement, sponsorship, authorization, procurement relationship, adoption, or operational integration is implied.

7. Evidence Requirement

Authorized affiliation must be supported by a retained record identifying:

organization;
representative;
authority;
date;
approved language;
scope;
expiration or termination conditions.
