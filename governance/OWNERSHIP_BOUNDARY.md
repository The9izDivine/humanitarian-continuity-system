# Ownership Boundary

## 1. Repository Owner

The Humanitarian Continuity System is independently developed and controlled through The9izDivine unless a later signed agreement establishes a different legal owner.

GitHub hosting location, collaborator access, pull-request activity, company participation, or public presentation does not independently determine ownership.

## 2. Separate Institutional Identities

The following are separate:

### ORYNTH

ORYNTH is the protected research, publication, governance, and intellectual-property institution associated with Ashley S. Harris and ORYNTH, Inc.

### NeoMythic Works

NeoMythic Works is a separate creative, product-development, and application identity.

### The9izDivine

The9izDivine is the originating GitHub development identity for this humanitarian prototype.

### Continuous Systems

Continuous Systems is a separate collaborative engineering and business organization.

## 3. No Automatic Transfer

No repository, concept, code, schema, document, test, model, specification, data structure, terminology, or design transfers between these institutions merely because:

- the same person contributes;
- a contributor is identified as a co-founder elsewhere;
- repositories are publicly visible;
- work is discussed in a shared group;
- a repository is shown to a collaborator;
- a system references similar principles;
- implementation concepts are structurally related;
- development occurs during the same period.

## 4. ORYNTH Reserved Property

ORYNTH Reserved Property includes all ORYNTH:

- repositories;
- publications;
- research frameworks;
- constitutional documents;
- mathematical formulations;
- specifications;
- taxonomies;
- registries;
- patents and patent-sensitive materials;
- source code;
- evidence systems;
- unpublished research;
- names, marks, and institutional identity.

No ORYNTH Reserved Property is assigned or licensed by this repository.

## 5. Project-Specific Collaboration

Any future collaboration with NeoMythic Works, Continuous Systems, ORYNTH, or an external organization must be governed by a written project charter or license identifying:

- participating parties;
- exact assets;
- ownership;
- permitted use;
- commercial rights;
- publication rights;
- attribution;
- confidentiality;
- termination;
- surviving rights.

## 6. Contributions

Unless a signed contribution or employment agreement states otherwise, contribution to this repository does not automatically transfer ownership of unrelated background intellectual property.

Every contributor must have authority to submit their contribution.

## 7. Reservation of Rights

All rights not expressly granted are reserved.
